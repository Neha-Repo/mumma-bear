# Mumma Bear

Mumma Bear is an Android-first motherhood companion designed to reduce mental load during the first year after birth.

The app focuses on helping a mother:

- understand what matters right now
- quickly log feeding, pumping, milk, diapers, appointments, vaccinations, and recovery information
- remember important details she should not have to keep in her head
- prepare for upcoming stages and tasks
- access low-effort, one-handed workflows designed for tired, real-life use

## Product principles

- Mom is central
- Low effort over feature density
- Context before content
- Short answer first
- Remember things for the user
- Prepare proactively
- Safety over confidence

## V1 scope

The first version includes:

- Onboarding
- Today
- Baby
- Me
- Quick actions
- Feeding
- Breastfeeding
- Pumping
- Milk tracking
- Diaper tracking
- Appointments
- Vaccinations
- Reminders
- Recovery check-ins
- Show Me How guides
- Stage-aware content
- Remember + Prepare
- Settings and appearance

AI is intentionally excluded from V1 and may be added later.
